Register your collectibles. Prove their history.

Chainpiece gives every object a permanent registration ID and a tamper-evident, hash-chained record of its documented history — photographs, ownership claims, and custody transfers.

Create your account

How it works

  1. Create a free collector account.
  2. Register an object with photos and details (date, mint mark, weight, distinguishing features).
  3. Receive a permanent registration ID (format CRN-XXXXX-XXXXX).
  4. Record its history. Every event is chained cryptographically to the one before it, so any tampering is detectable.
  5. Share the object's public profile page with other collectors.

Important: everything recorded here is a claim until verified evidence supports it. The site clearly separates unverified claims from verified records.
